# Code of conduct

All participants in repository discussions, issues, reviews, and project events must act respectfully. Welcome different backgrounds and experience levels. Give constructive feedback about work; respect privacy and personal boundaries.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, doxxing, spam, and retaliation are unacceptable. Maintainers may remove content, warn participants, restrict participation, or ban participants proportionately to the behavior and its impact. Reports should be evaluated fairly, with conflicts of interest disclosed and recused where possible.

Report concerns privately to repository maintainers through an available GitHub profile contact. Do not post private details in public issues. A dedicated confidential reporting contact and independent appeal contact have not yet been established; maintainers must establish these before community expansion. Share only information necessary to investigate, and do not promise absolute confidentiality. Appeals may be submitted to another uninvolved maintainer where available.
